**Facility scope:** `bc-dc-vancouver-01`, `ns-port-halifax-01`

## Heat Operations

When forecast highs reach 30 degrees C:

* Move outdoor loading to 05:00-11:00 and 18:00-22:00
* Verify refrigerated-container fuel reserves for at least 48 hours
* Confirm backup power for temperature-controlled cargo

When forecast highs exceed 33 degrees C for two days:

* Activate cooling shelters for yard staff
* Limit continuous outdoor work to 30-minute rotations

## Coastal Storm Overlay

When Environment and Climate Change Canada issues a coastal warning:

* Top up generator fuel
* Confirm cargo-lashing and flood-barrier inventories
* Move priority imports to the alternate Canadian port before closure windows

## Site Notes

* Vancouver has Fraser River, wildfire-smoke, and Pacific storm exposure
* Halifax has Atlantic storm-surge and high-wind exposure
* Coordinate cross-country rerouting with `qc-pkg-montreal-01` and `mb-dc-winnipeg-01`
