## Design Envelope

* Cooling-water demand baseline: 10,500 m3/day
* Heat-response threshold: 33 degrees C
* Priority loads: process cooling, clean areas, and critical controls

## Heat-Stressed Operating Modes

When the forecast maximum exceeds 33 degrees C:

* Enter Mode H1 and increase cooling-water quality checks
* Confirm municipal makeup-water availability
* Shift non-critical maintenance to overnight hours

When the forecast exceeds 35 degrees C for two or more days:

* Enter Mode H2 and pre-cool the process-water reservoir overnight
* Defer non-critical thermal loads
* Brief the manufacturing manager on potential production curtailment

## Failure Modes

* Loss of a cooling train during Mode H1 triggers Mode H2 immediately
* A provincial grid emergency prevents discretionary load growth
* A water restriction triggers the approved low-water production schedule
