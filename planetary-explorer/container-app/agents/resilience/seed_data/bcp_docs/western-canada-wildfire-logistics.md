## Trigger Criteria

Activate when any of the following occurs:

* A provincial fire-weather warning covers a facility corridor
* The 24-hour PM2.5 forecast exceeds 35 micrograms per cubic metre
* An active wildfire perimeter is within 40 km of a facility or route

## Operational Actions

| Risk | Threshold | Action |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Outdoor staff exposure | AQHI high risk | Move yard operations indoors and issue fitted respirators for essential outdoor work |
| HVAC particulate load | PM2.5 above 35 micrograms per cubic metre | Use MERV-13 or better filtration and increase inspection frequency |
| Road disruption | Highway closure within 80 km | Reroute through the lowest-risk open provincial corridor |
| Power-line risk | Provincial red-flag warning | Coordinate backup-power readiness with the serving utility |

## Prince George Logistics Hub

* Confirm fuel and water reserves before forecast wildfire weeks
* Pre-stage alternate routes to Vancouver and Edmonton

## Vancouver Distribution Centre

* Protect priority port inventory from smoke and ash ingress
* Coordinate transshipment alternatives through Winnipeg

## Stand-Down

Stand down after air quality remains below the high-risk threshold for 48 hours and no active perimeter threatens a required route.
