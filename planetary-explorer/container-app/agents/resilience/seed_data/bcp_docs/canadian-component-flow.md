title: Canadian Component Flow Continuity Plan
description: Synthetic 2026 continuity actions for Toronto, Saskatoon, Edmonton, and Montreal supply edges.
---

**Edge scope:** `on-fab-toronto-01` to `ab-asm-edmonton-01`, and `sk-sub-saskatoon-01` to `ab-asm-edmonton-01`

## Normal Mode

* Toronto components reach Edmonton in three days
* Saskatoon sub-assemblies reach Edmonton in one day
* Edmonton assembled units reach Montreal packaging in four days

## Heat Event

* Pull forward one day of Saskatoon buffer inventory
* Shift Toronto production away from peak grid hours
* Extend Edmonton test windows to absorb upstream delay

## Wildfire or Smoke Event

* Reroute affected western road freight through the lowest-risk open corridor
* Switch critical facilities to enhanced particulate filtration
* Defer planned outdoor maintenance until air quality improves

## Downstream Continuity

* Prioritize Montreal packaging for units serving Vancouver, Winnipeg, and Halifax
* Use Halifax imports as an alternate component source when western routes are constrained

## Decision Rights

* Disruption-mode rerouting requires the Director of Operations
* Any quality-control bypass requires engineering and quality concurrence
